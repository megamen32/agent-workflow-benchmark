## Business-first workflow benchmark

Ten deterministic bugfix tasks were run against four matched arms: plain Codex,
LHC business-first, Superpowers 6.2.0, and GSD 1.22.4.

- 40/40 valid cells; every arm passed 10/10.
- Plain Codex was the Pareto winner for this short local task class.
- LHC was the most efficient full workflow.
- The evidence asset contains all public receipts, summaries, manifests, and
  separately credential-redacted transcript archives.
- The accompanying SHA-256 verifies the complete bundle.
